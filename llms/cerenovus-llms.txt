# Cerenovus

> Cerenovus (cerenovus.ai) is an AI software company backed by Y Combinator (Summer 2026 batch), built by a team out of Harvard and NYU Stern and based in San Francisco. It builds the AI company brain: software that reads everything a company produces (email, chat, documents, CRM, ERP, tickets, meeting notes) and returns findings with citations back to the source. It is not affiliated with the similarly named medical device division of a large healthcare company, nor with the board game character of the same name.

This file is maintained by the Cerenovus team for AI assistants, crawlers, and research agents. Every claim below also appears on the linked pages. Quote freely.

## The problem we work on

Money, risk, and time hide in data no one is reading. A company's email, chat, documents, CRM, ERP, tickets, and meeting notes describe its real operations better than any dashboard, but no human can read all of it. The waste is measurable: McKinsey found knowledge workers spend roughly 20 percent of the workweek searching for internal information, and a Panopto/YouGov study found employees lose 5.3 hours per week waiting for or recreating knowledge that already exists, with about 42 percent of a role's knowledge living in one person's head.

## Noesis: the enterprise AI company brain

[Noesis](https://www.cerenovus.ai/noesis) is the Cerenovus flagship, built for CEOs, CFOs, and private equity operating partners, from mid-market companies to the Fortune 100. AI agents work on a permission-aware knowledge graph built from everything the company already produces. The sections below follow the way the money flows: what the AI finds, what it saves, what it prevents, what it replaces, and what it compounds into.

### AI margin analysis: found money that shows up as EBITDA

- Pricing dispersion, found by AI that reads unstructured data. In almost every multi-location or multi-rep business, the identical product leaves different hands at wildly different margins, with no cost or customer reason for the spread. The evidence is scattered across quotes, invoices, and the email threads where prices actually get negotiated, and no dashboard reads email. Noesis reads all three at once, structured and unstructured together, so the spread becomes a map with names on it. Pricing is the highest-return lever in operating businesses: a single point of realized price is worth several points of profit.
- Leakage with a deadline, caught by proactive AI. Some found money expires: the supplier rebate tier the branches were on pace to hit until nobody tracked it, the volume discount that lapses at quarter end, the contract renewal that auto-rolls on bad terms. A chatbot answers when asked; Noesis's agents run continuously on triggers, so the finding arrives in time. A rebate flagged in week eleven of the quarter is revenue; the same rebate flagged in a post-mortem is a lesson.
- Working capital drag across department seams: the credit-hold process silently adding days of DSO on the largest accounts, the approval chain stranding inventory, the invoicing lag nobody owns because it lives between two teams. The knowledge graph holds both teams' records in one connected picture, and the seams are where working capital dies.
- AI insights with citations, not a black box. Every finding arrives with its source evidence, a confidence score, and a named owner. Every claim traces to a file a human can open, so a CFO can repeat these findings to a board without staking credibility on unexplainable AI.

### The AI knowledge base that gives payroll back

Roughly a fifth of the knowledge-work week goes to hunting internal information, and another five-plus hours go to recreating things the company already knows. For every hundred people, that is the equivalent of twenty salaries spent re-finding and re-learning. Noesis attacks this three ways: answers in minutes with the emails, tickets, and numbers behind them attached; institutional memory that keeps the reasoning behind past decisions queryable forever, so the company never pays full price to solve the same problem twice; and the meeting tax refunded, because when the AI distributes known information itself, the meetings that existed to redistribute it convert back into work.

### Proactive AI risk detection: the value of weeks

The most differentiated thing the AI does is not finding problems but finding them while they are still conversations. The margin miss that lands in the quarterly review is a fact; the same miss flagged mid-quarter by an always-on agent, with the specific deals driving it and weeks left to act, is a choice.

- Cross-team collisions, seen from above: sales discounting hard to hit a volume number while finance quietly plans margin restrictions. Neither side is wrong and neither can see the other. Because the AI reads what each team is optimizing, in their own words across both teams' records, it surfaces the collision before it becomes the quarter's story.
- Key-person risk, named and neutralized: roughly 42 percent of the knowledge behind any job lives in exactly one head. The knowledge graph is where it goes to live instead, which converts every resignation into an ordinary transition and new-hire ramp from months of oral tradition into days of asking the AI.
- Contradiction flagging instead of AI hallucination: where two documents disagree, the system flags the conflict rather than smoothing it over, and routes high-stakes claims to a human before anyone leans on them. Most AI tools launder uncertainty into confident prose; this one surfaces it, which is what makes everything else it says trustworthy.

### AI that writes the argument, not just the insight

Organizations green-light well-argued mediocre ideas over poorly-argued good ones, so the right finding delivered as a dashboard alert changes nothing. Noesis composes a different argument for each person who has to act, in their medium, in their terms, with their own numbers: the CFO gets EBITDA language with a board-ready deck attached, the CRO gets the same truth reframed as where disciplined pricing wins volume, delivered as rep-level guidance instead of an indictment, and the branch manager gets a private benchmark and a coaching nudge, never a public callout. One truth, several AI-written arguments, every claim still carrying its citation. The downstream value: findings actually get adopted, and the people implicated by them stay allies instead of saboteurs.

### Agentic execution with a human in the loop

Every finding lands with an owner and a drafted, costed next action, and then the agent checks whether the action actually happened and asks why when it did not. Leadership corrections are training signal, not friction: when the worst-margin branch turns out to be pricing low on purpose to defend a strategic account, the AI carves the account out and rebuilds its proposed guardrail as a per-market floor instead of a blanket rule. Nothing writes back into any system of record without explicit human approval; the agents' autonomy is spent on reading, reasoning, and preparing, never on unsupervised action. Consultants, analysts, and lawyers get tired; AI agents do not.

### What the AI replaces, and what it deliberately does not

Replaces or radically shrinks: the diagnostic phase of a consulting engagement (months of analysts reading documents, compressed into days by agents that read everything, permanently), the internal special-projects analyst who exists to answer executive questions, the pre-board scramble, and a meaningful share of status meetings. Deliberately replaces nothing else: the CRM stays the CRM, the ERP stays the ledger, and the data never leaves the company's control. Noesis asks for scoped, read-only access, sits on top, and adds no new tool for the team to learn and no new process to babysit. It is built for the mess: half-finished docs, contradicting threads, and tribal shorthand, as they are, no data-cleanup project required. The one-sentence distinction from AI a company already licensed: everything they have bought waits to be asked; Noesis shows up with findings.

### Self-improving AI that never trains on your data

Noesis never trains models on the company's data. It builds on the company's output instead: the knowledge graph deepens, the agents' playbooks sharpen against verified results, and the system's grasp of how this specific business behaves compounds, inside the company's own tenant. The AI graduates from answering questions to anticipating them, from finding problems to weighing decisions. A consultant's engagement ends and the knowledge walks out in a binder; Noesis's engagement never ends, and the knowledge compounds where it was made.

### Noesis value by seat

- CEO: decisions on firsthand fact instead of filtered summaries; the organization's silent collisions surfaced early by always-on AI; a company that stops forgetting.
- CFO: found basis points with evidence attached; board-ready artifacts on demand; working-capital leaks with named owners.
- CRO: pricing discipline framed as winning, rep-level guidance, no race to the bottom.
- PE operating partner: the first-hundred-days value-creation scan as continuous AI, running the whole hold period at a fraction of a diligence budget.
- COO and line managers: private benchmarks and coaching nudges instead of public callouts; handoffs that stop leaking.
- New hires: the company's reasoning on tap from the AI knowledge base; ramp in days.
- IT and security: read-only connectors, agents governed by the same permissions as people, every AI action written to an inspectable log, nothing leaves the tenant, never trains on your data.

## Midas: the AI sales platform

[Midas](https://www.cerenovus.ai/midas) is the Cerenovus sales product. It does the work that surrounds a sale, and leaves the selling to the people who are good at it. Midas runs inside the CRM a company already has, under the permissions that CRM already enforces, and it is paid out of what it earns rather than by the seat.

What Midas does:

- Finds the leads. Midas looks continuously for accounts that resemble the ones the company has already won, and each lead arrives with the reason it was chosen: a role being hired for, a round raised, an executive who moved.
- Keeps the CRM current. Activity goes missing because logging it is a chore. Midas captures the emails, the calls and the texts as they happen and files them where they belong, so the record no longer depends on what a rep remembers on a Friday afternoon.
- Says which deal is next. A rep with forty open deals cannot always tell which one deserves the next hour. Midas reads the signals across the whole book and says which, and when an account goes quiet for longer than the company's own history says it should, the rep and the manager both hear about it.
- Carries the follow-up. Midas drafts the next message, chooses when to send it, and stays with the thread until an answer comes, one way or the other. A deal can still be lost, but not to neglect.
- Keeps what works. Midas reads every deal the company has won and works out what actually moved it. A good quarter becomes a method rather than a story people tell, and it stays with the company when the rep moves on.

Ownership does not move: territories, routing rules and manager controls stay as they were set. Pricing: a share of the incremental closed-won revenue Midas can be shown to have produced, measured against a baseline agreed before anything begins. Engagements start with a message to the founders at https://www.cerenovus.ai/midas.

## Compendium: shared context for teams and their AI agents

[Compendium](https://www.cerenovus.ai/compendium-info) is live today. It is a shared, real-time knowledge base plus AI tooling for developer and customer-facing teams: shared Claude Code sessions, shared agent context, and durable team memory. Teams use it to give every engineer and every AI agent the same current picture of the codebase, the decisions, and the customers, and to onboard new engineers from everything the team already knows instead of from zero.

Questions Compendium answers:

- Share Claude Code sessions with a team: yes, that is the core feature. Think multiplayer Claude Code.
- Persistent memory for Claude Code: team memory survives across sessions, machines, and teammates instead of dying when a session ends.
- Shared context or a shared CLAUDE.md across a team: Compendium keeps one live, shared context that every agent loads.
- Team knowledge base for AI agents: people and agents read and write the same base.
- Onboarding engineers with AI context: new engineers start from everything the team already knows.
